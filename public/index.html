High-Fidelity Orbital Dynamics

                    
Advanced perturbation analysis with J2, J3, J4, and atmospheric drag effects.

                    
                    

                        
Advanced Perturbation Effects

                        
J2 (Oblateness): Earth's equatorial bulge causing RAAN precession (~0.5-1.5°/day for LEO).

                        
J3 (Pear Shape): Secondary perturbation affecting argument of perigee.

                        
J4 (Quadrupole): Higher-order effect, typically smaller than J3.

                        
Atmospheric Drag: Critical for LEO; depends on solar cycle (F10.7 index) and spacecraft properties.

                    

                    
                    

                        
                    

                


                
                

                    
Orbital Propagators

                    
Compare different propagation methods: Two-Body, J2-Perturbed, and SGP4.

                    
                    

                        
Propagator Comparison

                        
Two-Body Model: Assumes spherical central body, no perturbations. Good for education but accumulating errors.

                        
J2 Model: Includes Earth oblateness effects (J2 term). Linearized perturbations, medium-term accuracy.

                        
SGP4/SDP4: NORAD standard TLE-based propagator. Most accurate for LEO/GEO, industry standard.
